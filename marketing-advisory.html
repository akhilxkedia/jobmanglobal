From choosing the right legal structure and completing every statutory registration, to building the HR, legal and compliance foundations that keep you protected — one team covers the full path from market entry to ongoing governance.

Part 1: Incorporation & Statutory Registrations

Market Entry: Getting Registered

Right structure, right registrations, no gaps. What trips most entrepreneurs up isn’t deciding to incorporate — it’s knowing which registrations are mandatory, which licenses apply to their specific business, and at what stage each one becomes relevant. We guide you through all of it, end to end.

What we offer

Legal Entity Incorporation

We help Indian residents and foreign individuals/companies establish the right business structure for their goals, compliance needs, and operating model — whether you're a resident entrepreneur, an NRI, or a foreign company entering India.

Our support includes:

For Indian residents & foreign nationals/entities

  • Private Limited Company (including wholly/partial-owned subsidiaries or JV)
  • LLP (Limited Liability Partnership) — including Foreign LLPs
  • Partnership Firm / Proprietorship setup (Indian residents)
  • Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV/SPE) via Private Limited or LLP Structures
  • Joint Venture (JV) structuring with domestic or foreign partners

For foreign companies specifically

  • Branch Office / Liaison Office / Project Office setup (RBI/FEMA approval support)
  • Subsidiary & Project Office (PO) setup for market entry or project execution
  • Resident director sourcing/nomination (mandatory for foreign-owned companies)

Common to all entity types

  • Name reservation, amendments, documentation & MCA filings
  • Registered office address & compliance mailbox setup

Mandatory Compliance Registrations

We ensure your business meets every statutory registration requirement from day one — not discovered one at a time as each becomes urgent.

We manage:

  • GST Registration
  • Provident Fund, LWF, ESI
  • Professional Tax (PT)
  • Shops & Establishment Registration
  • MSME / Udyam Registration
  • Import Export Code (IEC)
  • PAN & TAN
  • DIN and DSC
  • Labour Law Registrations
  • Start-up India
  • Corporate bank account opening support

Registration gets you legally operational. What comes next is staying that way — Part 2 covers the ongoing advisory that keeps your business compliant, protected, and current as regulations evolve.
