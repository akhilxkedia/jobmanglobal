Demystifying Jargon

Plain-English India Business Glossary

Indian business and employment regulations come with a dense forest of acronyms. Here is what every critical term actually means in plain business English — so you never make decisions in the dark.

Employment Model

EOR • Employer of Record

What it is: A legally registered Indian entity that becomes the official, legal employer of your India-based workforce on paper. Jobman handles employment contracts, monthly payroll, tax withholding (TDS), statutory benefits, and severance.

Why it matters: You gain immediate access to Indian talent without spending months incorporating a subsidiary or managing local statutory filings. You direct day-to-day duties while retaining 100% intellectual property.
Explore EOR Solutions →
Workforce Structure

PEO • Professional Employer Organization

What it is: A co-employment service model commonly utilized when a company already maintains a registered Indian entity but chooses to outsource HR administration, payroll processing, employee benefits, and risk management.

Why it matters: While internationally "PEO" and "EOR" are frequently used interchangeably, in strict Indian practice an EOR employs staff when you lack an entity, whereas a PEO co-manages HR when your entity already exists.
Compare Workforce Models →
Contractor Management

AOR • Agent of Record

What it is: A specialized intermediary service for compliantly engaging, vetting, and paying independent 1099/freelance contractors and service vendors in India.

Why it matters: Protects international clients against "disguised employment" misclassification under Indian labour law, manages TDS withholding on contracts, and processes vendor invoicing under GST rules.
Review Governance Matrix →
Strategic Hub

GCC • Global Capability Centre

What it is: A dedicated, client-owned offshore delivery center (formerly termed "captive center") that operates as a direct extension of your parent company rather than a third-party outsourced agency.

Why it matters: GCCs build high-value proprietary capabilities — from core software engineering and AI to financial analytics and R&D. India hosts over 1,750+ GCCs employing 1.9M+ professionals.
Explore GCC Setup →
Hybrid Setup

BOT • Build-Operate-Transfer

What it is: A three-stage entry framework where Jobman builds the facility and recruits the team (Build), manages daily operations and compliance (Operate), and transfers the entire turnkey entity, team, and assets to you at a predefined milestone (Transfer).

Why it matters: You achieve maximum speed and eliminate upfront setup risk while preserving the definitive right to full corporate ownership once your operation hits scale.
Explore BOT Framework →
Corporate Charter

MCA / ROC • Ministry of Corporate Affairs / Registrar

What it is: The Indian central government ministry (MCA) and its state-level regulatory registrar (ROC) that govern the incorporation, administration, and legal dissolution of companies and LLPs in India.

Why it matters: Every subsidiary incorporation involves filing SPICe+ forms with the ROC to obtain your Certificate of Incorporation, Corporate Identification Number (CIN), Director Identification Numbers (DIN), and statutory articles.
Explore Entity Incorporation →
Cross-Border Capital

FEMA / RBI • Foreign Exchange Law & Reserve Bank

What it is: The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) administers the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA), which regulates cross-border currency flows, Foreign Direct Investment (FDI), and overseas remittances.

Why it matters: Capitalising an Indian subsidiary or repatriating service revenues requires strict adherence to FDI reporting (FC-GPR filings on the FIRMS portal) within mandated statutory windows.
Explore Financial Governance →
Labour Compliance

EPF, ESIC, Gratuity & LWF • Statutory Benefits

What it is: India's mandatory employee social security framework: Employees’ Provident Fund (EPF - retirement), ESIC (health insurance), Payment of Gratuity Act (statutory gratuity after 5 years), and Labour Welfare Fund (LWF).

Why it matters: Timely monthly deposit and filing of statutory contributions are strictly audited. Jobman ensures 100% on-time execution, protecting client directors from statutory notice exposure.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions — Jobman Global Services

Why should a company work with Jobman Global instead of handling India entry independently?

Setting up in India involves navigating entity registration, employment law, tax structuring, compliance, real estate, and hiring — all within a regulatory environment that differs significantly from Europe. Jobman Global manages this process end-to-end, reducing the time, risk, and internal resourcing required to do it alone.

How long does it take to set up a GCC in India?

Setting up a legal entity typically takes 1 to 3 months, followed by hiring, infrastructure setup, and operational ramp-up. Timelines vary based on the chosen city, entity structure, and scale of the initial team.

Does Jobman Global only support Tier-1 cities like Bengaluru or Hyderabad?

No. While we support businesses across major Indian cities, we also help companies establish operations in emerging Tier-2 and Tier-3 hubs where talent quality is high and operating costs are substantially lower. We support clients nationwide across Tier-1 metros (Bengaluru, NCR, Hyderabad, Pune, Mumbai) as well as high-growth tech corridors (Coimbatore, Kochi, Mysuru, Jaipur, and Nagpur) based on domain talent depth and cost economics.

How does Jobman Global help choose the right city for a GCC?

Location selection is based on the function being set up (engineering, finance, customer support, etc.), talent availability in that domain, cost considerations, infrastructure readiness, and applicable state incentive programs — since different Tier-2 cities have developed different specializations.

When should a company use EOR instead of setting up its own entity?

EOR is typically used when a company wants to hire quickly without the time and cost of entity setup, when testing the Indian market before committing to a permanent presence, or for smaller teams where a full legal entity isn't yet justified.

Can a company transition from EOR to its own entity or GCC later?

Yes. EOR is often used as a first step to validate market entry, with a transition to a legal entity or full GCC once the business case and scale justify it. Jobman Global supports this transition as part of its service offering.

How is an ODC different from a GCC?

An ODC is usually narrower in scope (primarily engineering/development work) and operates under the provider's legal entity. A GCC is broader in function and is owned directly by the client company through its own legal entity or a Build-Operate-Transfer arrangement.

Who is legally responsible for compliance under each model?

The service provider (EOR or ODC operator) carries the legal compliance responsibility. Under a fully owned GCC or legal entity, the client company bears full legal and compliance responsibility, typically supported by Jobman Global's advisory services.

What are the cost differences between these models?

EOR generally involves lower upfront cost with ongoing service-based fees. A fully owned legal entity or GCC involves higher upfront investment (incorporation, infrastructure, compliance setup) but can offer long-term cost efficiency at scale. ODC costs are typically bundled into a service fee with the provider.

Does Jobman Global help with entity registration and compliance directly, or only advise?

Jobman Global provides end-to-end support — from strategic advisory and location selection through entity registration, compliance setup, hiring, and ongoing operational management, depending on the engagement model chosen.

What is Jobman Global's Europe Desk?

The Europe Desk is a dedicated point of contact for European companies exploring India, providing region-specific guidance on market entry, partnership structuring, and ongoing advisory support from within Europe.

How does Jobman Global support companies before they've made a firm decision to enter India?

Jobman Global offers early-stage advisory conversations to help companies evaluate whether India entry makes sense for their business, which model (EOR, ODC, GCC, or legal entity) fits their stage of growth, and what a realistic timeline and cost structure would look like.

Can Jobman Global provide a focused delivery team — especially AI developers and other IT domains?

Yes. Under a CoE/EOR model as well as ODC — a team that works exclusively on the client's project under Jobman Global's infrastructure.

I am not ready to incorporate in India yet but confident in the long-term. What shall I do?

A Build-Operate-Transfer (BOT) engagement lets you keep operating under our umbrella while we build toward a defined transfer point — so you get time to prove the business case with a guaranteed path to full ownership.

Note: Specific timelines, costs, and city recommendations vary by client situation. This FAQ is intended as a general reference; contact Jobman Global directly for guidance tailored to a specific company's requirements.
