Statutory Clarity: Primary vs. Principal Employer

Under Indian labor jurisprudence, Jobman Global acts as the Primary Employer — assuming 100% legal accountability for employment agreements, PF/ESI/PT statutory contributions, labor code compliance, and termination protocols. Your company operates as the Principal Employer — retaining complete operational command over daily tasks, sprint deliverables, performance KPIs, and intellectual property (IP) assignment.

Employment Governance & Responsibility

Who legally employs the worker? Clear roles, zero ambiguity.

In an Employer of Record arrangement, legal employment and operational management are cleanly divided to protect both the worker and the principal company under Indian labour jurisprudence.

Legal Employer of Record

Jobman Global's Responsibilities

  • Compliant Employment Contracts: Issues tripartite/Co-employment or permanent or contractual employment agreements adhering strictly to applicable Indian Central and State labour regulations.
  • Monthly Payroll & Banking: Computes gross-to-net pay, processes salary disbursements on agreed schedules, and distributes digital pay slips.
  • Statutory Compliance & Filings: Manages employer/employee deductions and deposits for EPF (Provident Fund), ESIC (health insurance), Professional Tax, and Income Tax (TDS / Form 16).
  • Statutory Benefits & Insurance: Oversees statutory Gratuity provisioning, maternity benefits under the Maternity Benefit Act, and group health insurance.
  • Compliant Offboarding: Manages notice periods, Full & Final (F&F) settlements, experience letters, and statutory release documentation.
Operational Principal

Your Company's Responsibilities

  • Candidate Selection & Compensation: You interview, select, and decide candidate compensation, bonuses, and role specifications.
  • Day-to-Day Task Direction: You assign daily tasks, manage sprint deliverables, define workflows, and set performance KPIs.
  • Work Culture & Collaboration: The employee integrates into your internal Slack, teams, daily standups, and corporate meetings.
  • Performance & Promotions: You conduct performance evaluations, decide salary revisions, role promotions, or role terminations.
  • Full IP & Work Product Ownership: All intellectual property, code, trademarks, and inventions are assigned 100% directly to your company through enforceable covenants.

Compliance Clarity

EOR vs. Agent of Record (AOR)

It is vital to distinguish employees from independent contractors to avoid severe statutory misclassification penalties under Indian law:

  • EOR (For Statutory Employees): Full legal employment under Indian labour law. Includes statutory benefits, PF, ESI, gratuity, paid leave, and income tax deduction at source (Section 192). Best for full-time core contributors.
  • AOR (For Independent Contractors): Commercial service contracts under Section 194J. Jobman verifies contractor autonomy, executes vendor agreements, processes invoices, and handles tax deductions without employment relationship or co-employment liabilities.

Practical EOR Cost Structure

Our EOR pricing is structured with 100% transparency — no inflated statutory markups, no hidden forex conversion penalties:

  1. Gross Employee Compensation: Agreed salary (Base + HRA + Allowances).
  2. Statutory Pass-Throughs (At Actuals): Employer EPF (12% of statutory wage), ESIC (3.25% if applicable), Gratuity accrual (4.81%), and state labour welfare funds.
  3. Jobman Global Service Management Fee: A predictable, transparent fixed monthly fee per employee (or agreed fixed percentage of gross payroll).
  4. Optional Direct Reimbursables: Laptop equipment provisioning, coworking desks, and health insurance policy top-ups billed at a nominal mark-up.

Strategic Decision Framework

Employer of Record (EOR) vs. Setting Up a Legal Entity in India

Compare speed, statutory compliance, capital commitments, and governance to decide which model best fits your current stage of India growth.

Evaluation Parameter Employer of Record (EOR) Wholly Owned Subsidiary (Legal Entity)
Time to First Hire 5–10 Business Days: Immediate onboarding through Jobman Global's existing infrastructure. 1–3 Months: Requires MCA incorporation, name approval, bank account setup, and tax registrations.
Upfront Capital Commitment Minimal: Only initial payroll deposit and standard monthly service fee. Substantial: Legal incorporation fees, initial capital infusion (FDI/FEMA filings), and statutory deposits.
Corporate & Tax Governance Zero Local Entity Overhead: Handled 100% by Jobman Global. No local corporate tax returns or board meetings. Full Entity Governance: Requires Resident Director, statutory audits, ROC filings, and transfer pricing documentation.
Permanent Establishment (PE) Exposure Substantially Mitigated: Appropriate for R&D, software engineering, and back-office support functions. Recognized Taxable Entity: Permanent corporate presence liable to Indian corporate tax and transfer pricing rules.
Ideal Team Scale & Purpose 1 to 15 Professionals: Best for market testing, rapid team ramp-up, and proof of concept. 20+ Professionals / GCC: Best for long-term operations, commercial customer invoicing, and capital asset holding.
Transition Path Seamless Contract Migration: Jobman Global facilitates structured novation of employment contracts from EOR to your own legal entity once scale justifies incorporation.
