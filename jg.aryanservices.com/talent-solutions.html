The Integrated Foundation

AI and data at every step, backed by real human judgement

Most recruitment still relies on keyword-matched resumes and gut-feel shortlisting. That works until it doesn't — mis-hires are expensive, slow to detect, and even slower to unwind.

Jobman Global builds every search on a data-led foundation — we validate that candidates are likely to perform and stay, not just look right on paper.

  • AI-powered sourcing — surfaces passive candidates matching your role's real requirements, building a wider funnel faster.
  • AI-assisted screening — filters for genuine fit before a candidate reaches your team.
  • Structured assessments — technical, cognitive or behavioral, evaluating demonstrated capability.
  • Predictive analytics — flags likely performance and retention outcomes before an offer is made.
  • Background verification (BGV) — built into the same process so onboarding starts with full confidence.

Recruitment Models

Six hiring models, one accountable partner

How you can engage us — flexible models built around how your business hires.

Managed Hiring

We source against your role specs and shortlist with precision while you keep the final decision — a trusted partner running the process, not replacing your judgement. Including Gig Hiring.

Training & Placement Program (TPP)

We train freshers on corporate process and culture fundamentals — and specific technical or subject-matter skills where required — before they join your team.

Recruitment Process Outsourcing

Hand us part or all of your recruitment function. Dedicated, trained resources work on-site or remotely to run hiring end-to-end.

Global Hiring

Jobman holds a license from the Government of India to source and place Indian candidates at global locations — from sourcing through visa processing to travel.

Strategic, Contingency & Executive Search

Strategic search for roles where fit matters more than speed, contingency search for time-sensitive needs, and executive search for leadership positions requiring a confidential, targeted approach.

Mass Hiring & Custom Drives

Organise hackathons, walk-ins, campus hiring, job fairs and intern sourcing, at scale — plus bespoke assessments, predictive analytics, manpower planning and budget forecasting.
