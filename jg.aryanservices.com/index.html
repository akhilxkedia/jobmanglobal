Before You Decide

Questions every CXO should ask.

1

For International Companies Entering India

  1. Should we test the Indian market through an Employer of Record or establish our own legal entity?
  2. Which entity structure and operating model best suit our activities, investment plans and long-term objectives?
  3. What regulatory, tax, employment and compliance obligations will apply to our India operations?
  4. What will be the complete cost of establishing, employing and operating in India—not only employee salaries?
  5. Which location offers the right balance of talent, cost, infrastructure and business incentives?
  6. How will recruitment, payroll, HR, accounting, compliance, workplace and ongoing operations be coordinated?
2

For Indian Entrepreneurs and Startups

  1. Which legal structure, registrations and licences are appropriate for our business?
  2. Do we have the right accounting systems, financial controls and reporting procedures in place?
  3. Have we established the necessary HR policies, employment documentation, payroll and employee processes?
  4. Are we meeting applicable tax, labour, corporate and statutory compliance requirements?
  5. Can we hire and manage employees across different Indian states while maintaining consistent processes and compliance?
  6. Is our operating structure designed to support growth without creating unnecessary cost or complexity?
3

For Existing Companies Operating in India

  1. Are multiple service providers creating fragmented execution, duplicated effort or accountability gaps?
  2. Are our HR, payroll, finance, legal and compliance functions properly connected?
  3. Do we have accurate financial reporting and clear visibility of costs, liabilities and statutory obligations?
  4. Are our employment practices and labour-law compliances aligned with the states in which we operate?
  5. Which processes should be streamlined, consolidated or outsourced before we scale further?
  6. Do we have one accountable partner coordinating execution across the services we engage?

Wherever you are in your India journey, asking the right questions helps create a clearer, compliant and more scalable path forward.
