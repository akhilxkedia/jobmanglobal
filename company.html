Our Story

Built on a simple insight

Jobman Global — founded in 2022 and incorporated in 2024 under the Indian Companies Act 2013 — was built on a direct operational reality: companies expanding or operating in India lose time, capital, and momentum when managing fragmented vendors across recruitment, payroll, legal structuring, and office facilities.

When each provider operates in isolation, statutory gaps open up and accountability dissolves. The result is confusion, delays and zero accountability. We set out to change that. We replace disconnected vendor handoffs with a unified operating partner accountable for outcomes across the entire corporate lifecycle.

When clients say, “I wish someone could take care of this too” — we aim to be the partner who already does.
